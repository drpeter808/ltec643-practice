Policies

  • Accommodations: This class welcomes all students. If you need accommodations, contact me privately or the KOKUA program (956-7511, kokua@hawaii.edu) to coordinate reasonable accommodations.
  • Course Platform: We will use Lamaku as a document repository, assignment submissions, and online class discussions.
  • Deadlines & Late Work: Assignments are due on indicated dates. Late assignments are accepted for credit only with instructor permission. Missing or late assignments will result in a decrease of points, including negative scores for non-submissions.
  • Time Commitment: Expect to spend a minimum of 5-10 hours on assignments each week. Highly successful students are good time managers.
  • Conduct & Academic Integrity: Follow the UH Manoa Student Conduct Code. All ideas used must be your own or properly credited. Plagiarism will result in failure and disciplinary action.
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI): AI is embraced to augment learning. Students are permitted to integrate AI into some substantive tasks (ideas, outlines, editing, proofreading, prototyping). Students are required to keep drafts of assignments and logs of interactions with AI applications to demonstrate their contribution. When in doubt, seek prior approval.
  • Collaborative Environment: Grades are based on standards; there is no limit to the number of A's and B's awarded. Students are not in competition and are encouraged to work together.
